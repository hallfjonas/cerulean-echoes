# 001 groove (SC): feedback

SuperCollider port of `tracks/house/80s/001-groove/001-groove.strudel` (frozen, decision D1). Same controls and texts.
Not yet checked by ear. Play it in the player (`001-groove`, engine SC) or with the `sound-tour` auto-performance, and
compare with `001-groove-strudel` where something sounds off.

## What to listen for

- Kick, bass, acid: same punch and filter movement as in Strudel; Master (column 8 fader) adds low end and drive.
- Gated snare: the short, dense 80s room (its own reverb, new impulse response); Gate makes the tail longer.
- Hats: the swing on row 3; Open (button 2) rings long on every off-beat.
- Break: Chop (row 3) and Stutter (button 2) stutter in 32nds, as in Strudel.
- Chords, pad, strings: the hall (new impulse response, room size 3.5) sounds about as big as in Strudel; Brightness on
  pad and strings now acts at once, also on held notes.
- Knobs act at once on sounding notes (Space, Fog, Thin, Tension); in Strudel they acted from the next note.
- Launchkey: keys as in 016; pad 11 is the orchestra hit.
