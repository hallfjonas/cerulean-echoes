# 002 groove (SC): feedback

SuperCollider port of `tracks/house/80s/002-groove/002-groove.strudel` (frozen, decision D1). Same controls and texts.
Not yet checked by ear. Play it in the player (`002-groove`, engine SC) or with the `dance-floor` auto-performance, and
compare with `002-groove-strudel` where something sounds off.

## What to listen for

- Kick Ghosts (row 3, 0.3 at load on a live controller): soft thumps between the beats.
- Rumble: the rolling low end in its own dark room. Motion only darkens it a little and does not breathe (that is
  how the Strudel track plays it, see `sc/PORTING.md`).
- Hats: a little swing always on, more with row 3.
- Percussion: TR-727 congas and bongos panning slowly; cowbell / shaker on button 1.
- Organ stab: all three layers ring with Decay (also the high one; as in Strudel).
- Tension: less reverb and echo than in 001 (only a third of the way to full room).
- FX: soft riser and tail, 16 bars by default.
- Knobs act at once on sounding notes (Space, Fog, Thin, Tension); in Strudel they acted from the next note.
