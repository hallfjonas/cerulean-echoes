# 017 groove techno (LSD): feedback

## Starting point: feedback on 016 (SC), 2026-11-20

- Wood: Rave was the best setting, with Brightness turned up. Don't just copy it, build on it.
- Percussion: Pillow needs a remake. The bongos should sound much more like real bongos.
- Strings: they sound dead.
- Stab: the setup is great, and the stab is the main character. 017 needs a different character, more LSD.
- Answer: a good second main character. Make it a little different, but keep it close to 016 so moving between
  the two tracks works.
- Accents and FX: keep them similar, but more techno, more space, more surprise, more variety, more LSD.

## What 017 does with it

- Wood: all three patterns build on Rave. Rave is the default. Spin plays the high block every third 16th (3 against 4),
  so it turns around the bar. Tumble adds flams and a mallet line over 8 bars. The low-pass now starts at 4 kHz (016:
  1.8 kHz), so the wood is bright with the knob at 0. A hollow wooden log doubles the low block, and every hit varies a
  little in strength.
- Percussion: a new voice, `sc/sounds/modalPerc.scd`. A strike excites tuned resonators. The drum skin's pitch drops
  slightly after the hit, and there is skin noise for slaps. The drums are macho C5, hembra F4 and conga C4, played
  with four strokes: open, slap, fingertip and thumb. Each drum sits in a fixed place in the stereo field. The timing
  is slightly loose, like hands. Complexity first fills the gaps with martillo fingertips and thumbs, then adds flams
  and 32nd rolls. Fill runs across the three drums.
- Strings: they follow the chords now (016 played a fixed F minor). They are bowed in a 3-3-2 pulse, or swell over
  2 bars when Bow is turned up. Filter and detune move on slow cycles. They can be heard from Tension 0 (45 %), and
  Tension brings them to full. Their level is +5 dB, about −27 LUFS alone, where 016 was −31 at Tension 0.6.
- Stab: an acid dub chord, built from root, third, seventh, ninth and a square an octave down. Every third hit is
  accented, and the accent moves through the figure from bar to bar. A note up to three 16ths after a different note
  slides in from it. Other notes drip down a quarter tone. The filter moves on two slow cycles (7 and 3 bars). There
  are 8 new figures, and figures 1 and 6 are 016's. The Shape column works as before.
- Answer: four of its eight rhythms are 016's. The blip now drips down a quarter tone, has a sine an octave above, and
  is panned wider.
- Accents: 9 shots (016: 5). Glass and metal use real FM now. New are a laser zap, a bleep run, an acid squelch and a
  wooden bell. One hit in six jumps an octave, and sines drift as they ring. The accents now have their own echo, so
  Space at 0 still has some.
- FX: the riser has a second, ringing band that falls against it. The tail is one of three, picked anew each phrase:
  016's noise and boom, a laser fall, or a wooden bell. Complexity at the top adds a dub siren. FX have their own echo
  now.
