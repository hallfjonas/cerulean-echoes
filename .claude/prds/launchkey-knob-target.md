# PRD: launchkey-knob-target

## Executive Summary

The Launchkey MK4 25 player gets more control over the drum pads and a keyboard view that looks like a keyboard.
A button on the Launchkey switches the 8 knobs (CC 31-38) between two targets: the keys (today's jobs) and the
drum pads (new jobs). The keyboard in the LC view (`▣ LC big`) is drawn like a real one: white keys touch each other,
black keys sit in the upper part and overlap both white neighbours.

## Problem Statement

- All 8 knobs belong to the keys. The 16 drum pads only get knob 8 (Pad Fog). The pad player cannot shape the
  one-shots (no level, space, echo, pitch of their own).
- The keyboard in the LC view is a grid of 25 equal cells with gaps: black and white keys are the same size, side by
  side. The player has to count cells to find a note.

## User Stories

1. **As the Launchkey player, I switch the knobs to the pads** with one button press, so I can shape the one-shots
   without a second controller.
   - One press switches Keys → Pads, the next press Pads → Keys.
   - After the switch the knobs change only the new target. The values of the other target stay where they were.
   - Turning a knob after a switch does not make an audible jump in the target it now controls.
2. **As the Launchkey player, I see which target the knobs have** at a glance in the LC view.
   - The Launchkey row shows the target (Keys / Pads) and the 8 knob names and values of that target.
3. **As any player, I find a note on the view's keyboard** as on a real keyboard.
   - White keys sit side by side with no gap; black keys are shorter, in the upper part, overlapping both white
     neighbours; scale (green/red) and pressed (bright) colours stay.

## Functional Requirements

- FR1: One Launchkey button (sends a CC on the MIDI port in the factory setting; which one is measured first) toggles
  the knob target between Keys and Pads. The target starts on Keys.
- FR2: Knob values are stored per target. Keys at start behave exactly as today (all knob defaults unchanged).
- FR3: Pads target: 8 knob jobs for the drum pads, defined in a lab round (not part of this PRD). Until then the Pads
  target carries today's Pad Fog on its knob 8 and placeholder jobs that are named in `@lk` tags.
- FR4: Knob takeover after a switch without jumps: if the knobs send relative values, each target simply keeps its
  own value; if absolute, pickup (a knob takes over once it passes the stored value).
- FR5: The LC view shows the target, and the knob row shows the active target's names (from `@lk` tags, per target)
  and values.
- FR6: Keyboard drawing: 15 white keys (25 keys from C) in one row without gaps, 10 black keys about 60 % of the
  height, centred on the border between their two white neighbours. The window still follows the octave played.
- FR7: The same behaviour in 007, 008, 009 and 010.

## Non-Functional Requirements

- No extra CPU when the Launchkey is idle: the target toggle is a stateful closure created once and tapped via
  `stateTaps`, like `cycleButton`.
- No change to the sound with knobs at their start values (prove with `/strudel-optimize`).
- Layout sheets and `.syx` stay generated by the hook; `@lk` tags gain a per-target form the generator understands.
- English labels, one short line per `@lk` text.

## Success Criteria

- A button press flips the target in the view within one tick; 10 presses in a row are all counted, also while the
  keys stem is gated.
- Switching targets and turning knobs never changes the other target's values (simulator test).
- `/strudel-optimize` shows the four tracks identical to before with the target on Keys and knobs untouched.
- The view's keyboard: no gap between white keys, every black key overlaps both neighbours (screenshot check).

## Constraints & Assumptions

- The Launchkey stays in its factory setting (no custom mode). Which buttons send CCs on `Launchkey MK4 25 MIDI Out`,
  and whether the encoders send absolute or relative values, is not measured yet; the first task measures it with
  `snippets/midi-test.strudel`.
- `midikeys` has no note-off; nothing here changes that.
- Set 011 is generated from frozen dual versions and does not get the change.

## Out of Scope

- The 8 knob jobs for the pads (own lab round).
- Per-pad settings (knobs editing a single pad).
- LED feedback on the Launchkey for the target.
- Set 011.

## Dependencies

- `snippets/midi-test.strudel` to measure the Launchkey button and encoder messages.
- `tools/lcxl-layout` (layout sheet from `@lk` tags), `tools/strudel-sim` (simulator test), `/strudel-optimize`.
