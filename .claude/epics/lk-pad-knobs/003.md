# Task: The eight pad jobs

## Description
Decide by ear what each bank B knob does for its two pads, then put it into every SC track.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] One short English name per job, the same in every track (`@lk pad.kN`, one line, about 50 characters)
- [ ] Starting proposal, to be changed by ear: 1 Pitch, 2 Brightness, 3 Space, 4 Echo throw, 5 Length, 6 Drive,
  7 Pan, 8 Level. Each job musical on both pads of its column (voice samples and drum hits differ)
- [ ] Knobs at 0: pads sound as today
- [ ] Ported to 007, 008, 009, 010, 016; levels checked with `/strudel-mix` rules (nothing clips at the extremes)
- [ ] The job decisions written into the epic or `docs/` before the epic is removed

## Technical Details
Lab round per `/lab-board` if the jobs need a comparison; otherwise try them in 016 with the player.
Pad sets differ per track (`lkPadNames`), so a job may need a per-track tweak, not a different name.
