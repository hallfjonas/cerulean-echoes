## Implementation notes (2026-11-21)
- Skill: `.claude/skills/sample-designer/SKILL.md`. Order: plan in three lines, setup `drum` / `synth` / `free`, values
  from the machine's formulas (table in the skill), audition, gate, report with what to listen for, export only on request.
- Gate: `npm run sample-lab -- check <file>` and `sclang tools/sc-test/sample-designer.scd` (sclang from
  `/Applications/SuperCollider.app/Contents/MacOS/sclang` when not on PATH; about 2 s). Copy-and-change goes through
  `npm run sample-lab -- save-as`.
- Tried: "a soft tom with a short noise tail" (`soft-tom`, setup drum) and "a felt-like piano with a soft breath, slow
  attack" (`felt-piano`, setup free: osc, osc, noise, filter, env, chorus). Both pass the check, load in SuperCollider
  (25 checks), and are listed under Lab in the player's catalogue (the player itself was not started). A render shows
  the tom falling about 50 dB in 0.3 s and the piano reaching its peak after about 0.1 s; how they sound is the user's call.
- Found while trying: the check caught an `about` of 61 characters; `notes` and `velocity` are read at the time of each
  hit, so they must sit on the rhythm's grid (one value per hit gives wrong notes); the Noise block has its own decay
  and does not follow the played note, a breath on a melodic sound is therefore a fixed colour.
