## Chords: what the sources say
- Parallel "one-finger" chords: one fixed minor shape (triad / m7) moved by its root, 16th hits off the downbeat,
  2-bar pattern grown to 4 bars by muting hits (UR – Timeline).
  https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/tutorials/the-theory-of-techno-parallel-chord-stabs/
- Dub stab (Diva patch): saw + square + noise ("chuff" the delays grab onto), short amp sustain, **slow filter
  attack** ("key to the sound"), highpass + mid cut, tape echo dotted 1/8 plus a free echo with high feedback, slow
  LFO filter on the delay return. https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/synth-secrets/dub-techno-synth-chords/
- Basic Channel style: sampled chord one-shot pitched down, bit-reduced, slowly moving bandpass, dotted-1/8 echo,
  ducked by the kick; plus a held chord chopped into rhythm by 5/16 echoes.
  https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/beat-dissected/basic-channel-style-dub-techno/
- Jeff Mills "The Bells": 3 oscillators tuned to a minor triad (one key = chord), short decay, warble.
  https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/synth-secrets/jeff-mills-the-bells-part-2/ (already the pings' sound)
- Diagnosis (guess): our chord pulse is a clean minor 9 without noise, without echo feedback, without moving filter.

## Lead: what the sources say
- In techno the "lead" is a motif, not a tune; a catchy tune = drifting out of techno.
  https://violetrecording.com/how-to-make-techno/ ; Robert Hood – Minimal Mind (one-note stab developing)
  https://6amgroup.com/articles/all/minimal-techno-music-guide-history-artists-tracks
- One-note square stab: resonant LP + filter envelope, zero sustain, triplets, soft clip, ping-pong delay; evolves by
  cutoff; an answer copy through resonant bandpass + distortion.
  https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/synth-secrets/techno-synth-stabs/
- Short snappy acid line (Robert Hood). https://www.musicradar.com/how-to/how-to-program-a-robin-hood-style-lead-synth-sound
- Tonal loop from one sample (gong one-shot, tight envelope, resonant bandpass, retriggered).
  https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/synth-secrets/one-sample-infinite-movement-building-a-techno-instrument-in-absynth-6/
- Polymeter: 3/16 or 5/16 loops against 4/4. https://alijamieson.co.uk/?p=9536
- Vocal chops as short processed stabs. https://create.routenote.com/blog/?p=17293
