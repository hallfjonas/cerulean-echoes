## Playing styles
- M1 "Organ 2" (Robin S, Nightcrawlers): rather a syncopated organ bass/riff, not held chords
  (musicradar.com/how-to/classic-house-organ-robin-s-show-me-love; gijs.substack.com/p/the-legendary-korg-m1-organ-2-preset).
  Chicago/deep organ = held (Can You Feel It).
- KC stabs: without root, often without fifth, seventh at the bottom, bass and stabs alternating, change 1/16 early.
- Strings/pads: Solina (pulse width 5–20 %, filter sweep = "aah/ooh"), Juno (saw + sub square, chorus)
  (soundonsound.com/techniques/perfect-pads; musicradar.com/how-to/synth-string-synth-choir; attackmagazine analogue-style-string-synthesis).
- Live variation: add the 11 or 9, change the rhythm in bars 4 and 8, short melodies from shared notes between the stabs.
