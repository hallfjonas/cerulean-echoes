# Visuals

## What it is
The player draws the music while it plays. Every voice of a track (a stem) is shown with its name. Where it sits tells
you something about the sound: left to right is the stereo position (pan), low to high is the pitch, and the
brightness follows the loudness. A ring pulses with the kick drum and the tempo comes from Strudel's own clock.

## When to use it
For rehearsal, for a projector behind you, or to see which voice is doing what.

## How to use it
1. Start the [player](player.md), press Play and pick **Visuals** in the sidebar.
2. Open **Settings** (the gear in the sidebar) and find **Visuals**; **S** hides the sidebar. Press **F** for fullscreen.
3. Press **V** to change the look:
   - **deck** (default): glowing comets with tails, rings from the kick, axes.
   - **ocean**: the stems as liquid layers, time going into depth, a floating camera.
   - **clean**: plain comets and rings.
4. Use the two sliders in Settings, Visuals:
   - **Hard to Soft**: sharp and quick at 0, round and slow at 1.
   - **Raw to Smooth**: how much the picture averages over time. The sound is never changed.
5. Switches with keys: **A** axes, **L** labels (stem names), **B** beat rings, **W** pan width (**H**, the frame around the controller page
   you moved last, is in the MIDI panel section).

## Good to know
- Visuals change only the picture. The sound is untouched.
- Settings are kept in your browser.
- In this view the top bar hides after 3 seconds without the mouse.
- The Live page (the player's start page, or the window `▣ LC big`) shows both modes of the Launch Control and the
  Launchkey row.
- Details and the data format: `tools/trajectory/README.md`.
