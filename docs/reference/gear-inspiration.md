# References: gear we take ideas from

Hardware we don't own (and mostly don't plan to buy), but whose ideas we rebuild as machines in the SC Sound Lab.
Per device: what it is, and what we take from it. The machines themselves are planned in
`sc/PLAN-STEM-LIBRARY.md` (table "Maschinen").

Rule: we copy what a device does, not its panel. The layout stays our LC scheme; the feel of the original
(pins, sliders, touch plates) is the part we can't copy.

## Synths

### Cyma Forma RND
- One-button desktop synth: every press generates a new idea, up to 4 instrument tracks, each with its own random
  patch and sequence (Euclidean rhythms, random scale and time signature). 8 engines: subtractive, FM, acid, noise,
  speech, Karplus-Strong, supersaw, additive. About 125 € + VAT.
- Take: the "Würfel" mode for every Stage-1 machine (roll, keep, back). Purely algorithmic, so fully rebuildable.
- https://www.soundonsound.com/news/cyma-forma-bambounou-unveil-rnd-synth

### Cyma Forma ALT
- Analog "soundscape synthesizer" for drones and generative melodies: 5 voices on faders, pin-matrix modulation
  (2 LFOs, random step, envelope follower), scale quantizer, stereo delay with near-infinite feedback, light and
  sound sensors as modulation. About 1,279 €.
- Take: Texture machine with a mod matrix, quantizer and feedback delay; per-voice drift and saturation to
  approximate the analog behaviour; mic (and maybe light) as modulation, mainly for installations.
- https://www.gearnews.com/cyma-forma-alt-synth/

### GLX Balsam
- 8-voice "modal" polysynth without a keyboard: 8 sliders, each the pitch of one voice; slow polyphonic glide
  ("viscosity"), inspired by pedal steel and the long portamento of OB-8 / CS-80; just and equal intonation;
  second mode turns the sliders into a polyrhythm sequencer. About 2,399 €.
- Take: Balsam machine on the LC's 8 faders (one voice per fader, glide time on a knob, just intonation).
- https://synthanatomy.com/2025/12/glx-balsam-a-reimagined-modal-polysynth-playable-with-eight-pitch-benders.html

### Elta Music Solar 42
- Analog microtonal drone / ambient machine (successor of the Solar 50): 8 voices, 6 of them drone voices (the
  "classic" drones have 5 oscillators in different ranges each), 2 melodic voices on a touch plate with pressure;
  dual Polivoks-style filter, photo sensors, step sequencer, joystick, effect cartridges. About 2,000-2,300 €.
- Take: drone voices as stacks of 5 oscillators, held open by buttons; microtonal tuning; pressure as a control
  (fits a touch controller like the Erae Touch).
- https://www.perfectcircuit.com/signal/elta-solar-42n-review

## Sequencers and samplers

### Torso T-1
- 16-track algorithmic MIDI sequencer built on Euclidean rhythms, with per-step algorithmic editing.
- Take: model for our note and rhythm machines (Euclid, Arp, Grids): few knobs that reshape a whole pattern.
- https://synthanatomy.com/tag/torso-electronics

### Torso S-4
- 4-track "sculpting sampler": tape-machine workflow, deep granular engine (OS 2.2), real-time processing of
  samples and live input.
- Take: Sampler machine (vocal pads) and FX (granular freeze, tape) as a chain per track.
- https://synthanatomy.com/2024/01/torso-electronics-s-4-a-modern-tape-machine-with-advanced-sound-sculpting-features.html

## Touch instruments

### Plinky 12 Chords (Making Sound Machines)
- "Harmonic inspiration machine": 16x16 grid of pressure-sensitive touch pads, own 12-voice synth engine (6 voices
  chords, 6 melody), surfaces for chord notes and suggested related chords, 128-step sequencer for chord shapes;
  CV and MIDI over 12 TRS jacks; swappable panels (Blocks, Chords, Toadstep share one hardware). About $619.
- Take: harmony suggestions (related chords as a playable surface) for the Harmonie-Zentrale and Chord Memory;
  maybe as a touch controller that sends ready-made chords to SC (check the manual for what its MIDI out sends).
- https://www.perfectcircuit.com/plinky-plinky-12-chords.html
